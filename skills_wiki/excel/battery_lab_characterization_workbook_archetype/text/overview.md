# Battery Lab Characterisation Workbook Archetype

Builds a research-style battery cycling workbook with `Raw_Cycles`,
`Cell_Metadata`, `Derived_Metrics`, and `Summary_Plots`. The mechanism is
noise-controlled generated lab data showing gradual capacity fade, followed by
formula-backed normalised capacity and fade-rate metrics.

Use this for cell cycling, materials characterisation, degradation analysis,
capacity retention, internal resistance, and coulombic-efficiency briefs.
