# CFO Scenario Board Workbook Archetype

Builds a finance workbook with `Inputs`, `Revenue Build`, `Cost Model`,
`Scenario Matrix`, `KPI Dashboard`, and `Board Summary`. The mechanism keeps
assumptions centralized, derives monthly forecast and opex formulas, and uses
a scenario matrix for board-ready base/upside/downside comparison.

Use this for AI infrastructure revenue models, CFO planning, board packets,
scenario matrices, and KPI dashboards.
