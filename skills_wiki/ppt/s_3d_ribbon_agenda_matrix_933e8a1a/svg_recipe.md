# SVG Recipe — 3D Ribbon Agenda Matrix

## Visual mechanism
A dense agenda is transformed into a premium matrix by giving each item a “wrapped ribbon” construction: a colored tab sits behind a white rounded card, with a darker triangular fold creating the 3D illusion. A gradient number node overlaps the seam, making each agenda item easy to scan while preserving a polished executive-slide feel.

## SVG primitives needed
- 1× `<rect>` for the cool gray slide background
- 12× `<rect>` for colored ribbon tabs behind each agenda card
- 12× `<path>` for dark triangular fold shadows that create the 3D wrap effect
- 12× `<rect>` for white foreground agenda cards with rounded corners and soft shadows
- 12× `<circle>` for gradient-filled numbered anchor nodes
- 37× `<text>` elements for the slide title, agenda numbers, rotated “S T E P” labels, and agenda content
- 12× `<linearGradient>` definitions for the circular node highlights
- 1× `<filter id="cardShadow">` using `feOffset`, `feGaussianBlur`, and `feMerge` applied to the white agenda cards

## Avoid in this skill
- ❌ Using `<use>` to duplicate agenda cards; repeat the editable SVG shapes directly instead.
- ❌ Applying `clip-path` or `mask` to card shapes for the fold effect; use a simple editable `<path>` triangle.
- ❌ Putting a filter on `<line>` elements; shadows should be applied to the white `<rect>` cards only.
- ❌ Building the matrix as a flat table with borders; the visual impact depends on overlapping tabs, folds, shadows, and nodes.
- ❌ Overcrowding each card with long paragraphs; this layout works best with one bold title and one short explanatory line.

## Composition notes
- Keep the matrix in two columns with generous gutters: the white cards should feel like separate floating panels, not a spreadsheet.
- Reserve the upper 90 px for the title and subtitle; begin the first row around y=110 to prevent visual crowding.
- The colored tab should sit slightly up and left of the white card, while the dark triangle must touch the card’s left edge to sell the folded-ribbon illusion.
- Use a varied but balanced color rhythm across rows; avoid placing very similar hues directly opposite each other unless the agenda has a deliberate grouping logic.
